EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT AND RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

1. NON-COMPETE AND INDUSTRY RESTRICTION
For a period of 24 months following the termination of employment for any reason, Employee agrees not to work for, advise, consult with, or hold equity in any business operating in the software, tech, or digital services sector worldwide.

2. NON-SOLICITATION OF CLIENTS AND STAFF
Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, contact, or entice away any customer, client, vendor, employee, or contractor of the Employer for 36 months following departure.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
Employee hereby assigns to the Employer all rights, titles, and interests in any invention, software code, design, patent, or idea created, conceived, or authored by Employee, whether created during working hours or personal free time, and whether or not related to the Employer's business.

4. NOTICE PERIOD AND TERMINATION
The Employer may terminate Employee's employment immediately at any time for convenience without prior notice or severance pay. Employee must provide at least 6 months written notice prior to resignation.

5. OVERTIME AND SALARY DEDUCTION
Employee agrees to work all necessary overtime without additional compensation or pay in lieu. Employer reserves the right to deduct from Employee's wages or final paycheck any amounts deemed owed for training costs, equipment damage, or operational losses.

6. PROBATIONARY PERIOD
Employee shall serve a probationary period of 12 months, during which time Employee may be dismissed at any time without notice, cause, or explanation.
