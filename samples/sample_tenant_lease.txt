RESIDENTIAL LEASE AND TENANCY AGREEMENT

1. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant agrees to pay a security deposit of $3,000 upon execution. Landlord reserves the absolute right to retain the entire security deposit as liquidated damages if Tenant terminates the tenancy prior to the expiration date for any reason whatsoever.

2. RENT REVIEW AND INCREASE
Landlord reserves the right to increase the monthly rent at any time during the tenancy upon 7 calendar days written notice. Rent increases shall not be capped or restricted to any inflation index or statutory limit.

3. LANDLORD RIGHT OF ENTRY
Landlord, its agents, and prospective buyers or tenants may enter the premises at any hour of the day or night without prior notice for any inspection, repair, or showing purpose.

4. SUBLETTING AND OCCUPANCY
Subletting, assignment, airbnb hosting, or permitting any guest to reside on the property for more than 28 consecutive hours is strictly prohibited and constitutes immediate grounds for eviction and forfeiture of deposit.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR OBLIGATIONS
Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, repairs, plumbing fixes, structural maintenance, and roof repairs exceeding $50 per occurrence, regardless of cause or normal wear and tear.

6. LATE FEES AND PENALTIES
Rent is due on the 1st day of each month. A fixed late fee of $200 plus 15% compounded daily interest will be assessed on any rent payment received after 11:59 PM on the 1st of the month.
