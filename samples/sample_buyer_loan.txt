LOAN AND CREDIT AGREEMENT

1. INTEREST RATE CALCULATION AND VARIATION
Borrower agrees to pay interest at an initial rate of 12% per annum. Lender reserves the unrestricted right to adjust, increase, or double the applicable interest rate at any time upon 3 business days notice.

2. EARLY REPAYMENT PENALTY
If Borrower prepays or settles the outstanding loan balance prior to the maturity date, Borrower shall pay an early termination penalty fee equal to 10% of the original principal amount.

3. DEFAULT FEES AND ACCELERATION
Any payment default lasting more than 48 hours shall trigger immediate acceleration of the total loan balance. A default fee of $500 plus default interest of 24% per annum shall apply immediately upon default.

4. SET-OFF AND RIGHT TO SEIZE BALANCES
Lender retains a general lien and right of set-off over all bank accounts, deposits, funds, and assets held by Borrower with Lender or its affiliates, allowing Lender to seize funds without notice to satisfy alleged obligations.

5. WARRANTY DISCLAIMER AND FAULTY GOODS
Borrower acknowledges that Lender makes no warranty or representation regarding the merchantability, fitness for purpose, or condition of financed equipment. Borrower's obligation to repay loan instalments remains absolute even if financed goods are defective or broken.
